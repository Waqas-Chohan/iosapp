# Changelog

All notable changes to Muz. Newest first.

## 1.0.89 — 2026-11-09

### ✨ New
- Muz Wrapped plays as stories: tap through your month with numbers that count up and colours from your top song.
- A new Muz Wrapped picture on Home that matches your theme.
- The player background slowly moves with colours from the song's cover.
- Lyrics: the current line glows in the cover's colour, the others softly blur.
- Pinch or double-tap the cover in the player to see it full screen.
- The cover grows smoothly from the mini player into the full player.

### 🛠 Improved
- Shimmering placeholders instead of spinners while songs load.
- You tab: your account is back on top; Activity moved under Notifications.
- Profile: the "Your name" label sits neatly above the box.

### 🐞 Fixed
- Swiping the mini player up now opens the player on the first try.
- Search: removed the extra small ✕ inside the search box.

## 1.0.87 — 2026-11-06

### ✨ New
- Listen Together pop-ups: see when friends join or leave, and invite everyone in the playlist with one tap (they get a notification with Join).
- Download all: one button at the top of every playlist downloads the songs you don't have yet.
- Pin playlists to the top of Library and Home (playlist ⋯ › Pin).
- Choose your own cover for shared playlists too.
- Share a playlist straight to Muz friends by email or name.
- The player background takes its colours from the song's cover.

### 🛠 Improved
- Player: a short, bold song name on the left (Spotify style); the bars stop when the song is paused.
- Songs you download now play from the download inside your playlists.

### 🐞 Fixed
- Listen Together: listeners can't accidentally change the song any more (a message explains why), and Android listeners now follow the host's songs even when Muz is in the background.

## 1.0.85 — 2026-11-06

### 🐞 Fixed
- Android: Muz closed straight away (“Muz keeps stopping”) in 1.0.83 and 1.0.84. It opens normally again.

## 1.0.84 — 2026-11-06

### ✨ New
- Import playlists (Premium): Library › ⋯ › Import playlist. Paste a Spotify link (from Spotify or WhatsApp) or choose a playlist file (CSV from Exportify / TuneMyMusic). Muz finds every song on YouTube and lets you check the matches before saving.

## 1.0.83 — 2026-11-06

### ✨ New
- Invite people to a shared playlist by their email or name (they get an invite with Accept / Decline)
- You › Activity: invites, friends playing your playlists and new songs they add
- Notifications for shared playlists, with switches for each kind and muting of people or playlists

## 1.0.81 — 2026-11-02

### ✨ New
- A brand-new Search page: colourful category tiles with artist photos (they stay on your phone for offline), tap one to search it
- Search mode: the bar slides up, a round ✕ appears, and you can switch between Online and your Library; recent searches are remembered
- Voice search: tap the mic and say a song or an artist
- The mini player is now a floating glass bar: drag it up to open the full player, swipe the full player down to shrink it back, swipe the bar left / right for next / previous

## 1.0.80 — 2026-11-02

### ✨ New
- Heart any song: it goes straight into your new Liked Songs playlist (with a little vibration)
- Swipe a downloaded song or video to the left to delete it from Muz and Photos (asks first)
- Your own covers: pick a photo as the cover of any song or playlist (⋮ menu)
- You › Customize: app icons (Premium), glass transparency, Muz logo colour, Wrapped on Home on / off and the starter animation on / off
- New video player: tap for controls, ±10 seconds, heart and ⋮ menu, and a proper full screen with a seek bar, loop, background and queue

### 🛠 Improved
- Menus and questions are now frosted glass and sit flush with the bottom of the screen
- Song covers are no longer zoomed in
- Tapping Home takes you back to the top of Home
- Swipe a finished download to the left to clear it from the Downloads list

### 🐞 Fixed
- Videos no longer pause for a second when you leave the app (they carry on in the Dynamic Island)
- Android: swiping back from the edge of the screen no longer freezes the page

## 1.0.77 — 2026-10-31

- HD video downloads (merged video + sound) finish properly too

## 1.0.76 — 2026-10-17

## 1.0.75 — 2026-10-16

### ✨ New
- Optional Muz account: sign in with Google or email + password (You › Account)
- Muz Premium (Rs 199 a month or Rs 1,499 a year): unlimited downloads, HD video, equalizer, crossfade, Listen Together hosting, Wrapped sharing and 5 devices

- Account screen: your plan, your devices (sign one out remotely), change password, delete account

## 1.0.74 — 2026-10-16

### ✨ New
- Welcome screens for new users: pick favourite artists and moods so Home has music for you from day one
- Muz Wrapped: your top songs, top artists and minutes each month, as a picture you can share
- Listening history and a Recently played row on Home
- Smart Radio: when your queue ends, Muz keeps playing similar songs
- Crossfade (0–12 seconds) and gap-free start of the next streamed song
- Equalizer with Bass Boost, Vocal, Qawwali, Treble, Pop, Rock and Night presets, plus your own curve (Android)

- Android home-screen widget with artwork and play / pause / next / previous

### 🛠 Improved
- Clear "No internet", "Song unavailable" and "No results" screens with a Try again button
- An offline bar shows when there is no connection (downloads still play)
- Crash reports help fix problems faster
