# Changelog

All notable changes to Muz. Newest first.

## 1.0.73 — 2026-10-15

### 🐞 Fixed
- **Android:** downloaded songs and videos now play (they stayed paused before).
- **Android:** you feel a short vibration when you add a song to the queue.

## 1.0.72 — 2026-10-15

### 🛠 Improved
- **Android:** a clearer, more see-through glass tab bar that floats over the content, edge to edge.

### 🐞 Fixed
- **Android:** songs now play — downloaded and streamed songs no longer close the app.
- **Android:** fixed "App not installed" on some phones (such as Samsung Galaxy on Android 11); the download is also about half the size.

## 1.0.71 — 2026-10-15

### 🐞 Fixed
- **Android:** fixed "App not installed" on some phones (such as Samsung Galaxy on Android 11); the download is also about half the size.

## 1.0.70 — 2026-10-15

### ✨ New
- **Muz for Android** — the full app on Android phones, with playback controls in the notification and on the lock screen. Download **Muz.apk** below.

### 🛠 Improved
- **Mac:** music keeps playing when you close the window — control it from **Control Center › Now Playing**, the media keys, or by right-clicking Muz in the Dock. Click the Dock icon to bring the window back; quit with ⌘Q.

## 1.0.68 — 2026-10-15

### ✨ New
- The official Muz website — [muzwebsite.vercel.app](https://muzwebsite.vercel.app) — linked from **You › About**.

### 🛠 Improved
- "Check for updates" now opens the Muz website for install help.

## 1.0.67 — 2026-10-15

### 🛠 Improved
- Updates now come from the official Muz download page, with checksums for every file.
- Faster, more reliable start-up checks.

## 1.0.65 — 2026-10-14

### ✨ New
- **Listen Together** — join a shared playlist session and hear the same song at the same moment as your friends.
- Developer credits with Instagram link in **About**.

### 🛠 Improved
- Home carousel shows three full cards on wide screens (Mac).
- About › "What you can do" cards redesigned.

## 1.0.64 — 2026-10-13

### ✨ New
- **Muz for Mac** — one universal app for Apple chips and Intel, with sidebar, menu bar and keyboard shortcuts.

## 1.0.57 — 2026-10-13

### ✨ New
- **Dark mode** — follow the iPhone or pick Light / Dark yourself.
- Profile picture (avatar) on the **You** tab.
- **Stream** button in search results — play instantly without downloading.
- Recently streamed songs in your library.
- **About** page.
- Artist pictures available offline.

## 1.0.56 — 2026-10-13

### ✨ New
- **Shared playlists** — share a playlist with a code; friends can join and edit together live.

## 1.0.55 — 2026-10-12

### ✨ New
- Versioned releases with a SideStore / AltStore source.
- **You › About › Check for updates** and an update badge on the **You** tab.
