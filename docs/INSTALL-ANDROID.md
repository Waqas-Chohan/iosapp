# Install Muz on Android

Android 6.0 or newer. No computer and no account needed, and the app never expires.

## Install

1. On your phone, open <https://muzwebsite.vercel.app> and tap **Get it on Android**
   (or download [Muz.apk](https://github.com/Waqas-Chohan/iosapp/releases/latest/download/Muz.apk) directly).
2. Open the downloaded **Muz.apk** from the notification or your **Downloads**.
3. If Android says your browser isn't allowed to install apps, tap **Settings**, turn on
   **Allow from this source**, then go back.
4. Tap **Install**, then **Open**.
5. When Muz asks to send notifications, tap **Allow** — that's how the playback controls
   appear on your lock screen and in the notification shade.

## Updates

Open **Muz → You → Check for updates** and tap **Download**. Open the new APK and tap
**Update**. Your library, playlists and settings are kept.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| "App not installed" | You may have an older test version with a different signature. Uninstall Muz, then install again. |
| Google Play Protect warns about the app | Tap **More details → Install anyway**. Muz isn't on the Play Store, so Play Protect doesn't recognise it. |
| No controls on the lock screen | Settings → Apps → Muz → Notifications → turn them on. |
| Music stops in the background | Settings → Apps → Muz → Battery → **Unrestricted**. |
